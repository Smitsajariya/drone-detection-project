How this works. The browser sandbox can't download an AI model, so detection runs on motion + shape, fully in your browser. Auto-detect (default for uploads) finds the drone by itself — it spots the small moving blob that stands out against the sky and boxes it, no clicking. It works best on clear-sky clips; busy backgrounds or a drone that holds still can fool it, and it can't tell a drone from a bird. Switch to Click-track to lock the box by hand. For true neural detection (drone vs bird, any background) you need a drone-trained YOLOv8 run in Python — ask and I'll build that. Nothing you upload leaves your browser.

AI mode. Press ◆ AI mode to switch from motion detection to a drone-trained YOLOv8 neural network (ONNX, runs locally in your browser via onnxruntime-web; the model downloads once). It recognises the drone by its shape, so it also works close-up, indoors, against busy backgrounds and with a moving camera. Works on the sample, uploaded clips and the live camera.

Live camera. Press ● Live camera and allow camera access (phone: uses the back camera). Put the camera on a tripod or still surface pointed at the sky — auto-detect looks for small moving targets, so a shaking camera causes false alarms. Raise Detail for far-away drones, switch Target to bright at night, lower Sensitivity if birds/leaves trigger it. Camera frames are processed locally and never uploaded.
